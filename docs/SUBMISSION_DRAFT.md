## Project name
DreamShield — Perps Protection Desk

## Tagline
Know whether your DreamDEX perps position has a native stop loss, and repair gaps with evidence.

## Problem
A position can outlive its intended protection. A cancelled, partial, incorrectly directed or stale stop can look reassuring without covering the current position. Transaction success alone does not establish present protection.

## Solution
DreamShield separates stop discovery from authoritative readback. A focused desk explains missing SL coverage, prepares an owner-native reduce-only stop, journals the attempt before wallet approval and reconciles its canonical receipt and current state. Replay is explicit, stale data revokes green conclusions and unknown outcomes do not trigger blind resubmission.

## DreamDEX integration
Pinned `@somnia-chain/markets-sdk@0.32.1`, Somnia Tokyo chain 50383, native perps position and stop registry integration. The final Node v24.22.1 [doctor](../evidence/g0/doctor-2026-10-27T05-32-53.000Z.json) passed **transport-and-verified-market / exit 0** on 2026-10-27T05:32:53.000Z, diagnosing BTC/USDso with no signing/send; [log](../evidence/tests/mvp-doctor-final-2026-10-27.txt). Owner NOT_CONFIGURED/G0 false and accepted owner pool null remain; actual trading lifecycle is **BLOCKED_EXTERNAL / G1 NOT_RUN**. SDK read-only integration is not a successful executed order or permission to size above the locked market-minimum (~13 grid lots) or to sign G1 without an owner isolated EOA.

## Intended users and repeat use
An isolated testnet perps owner checks protection after opening or changing a position, after cancelling a stop, or when returning to the terminal. First value is a clear missing/partial/stale protection explanation without treating receipt success as current coverage. Repeat value is rechecking the position–native-stop relationship and preserving evidence after changes. No paid demand or retention is claimed from replay tests.

## Implemented scope
One isolated account and one doctor-verified market per local instance; explicit replay, live-readonly and gated local-owner modes. Bigint assessment, whole-position sentinel handling, native SL/cancel adapters, bounded discovery/pinned risk reads, durable journal, unsigned previews and evidence export are implemented. Current offline tests, built replay, synthetic no-wallet capture/restart/export and CLI path ingestion have [scoped actual evidence](../evidence/mvp-2026-10-27/NOTES.md), not real owner execution. Local capture records existing opening/missing-SL/gap/residual observations; file-only checkers validate consistency without signing/current-chain revalidation. OCO relationship assessment exists; pair creation, relinking old legs, multi-market, Reactivity/Agents and opening execution are not claimed. G1 remains NOT_RUN.
