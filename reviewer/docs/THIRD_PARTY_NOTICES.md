# Third-party provenance

DreamShield depends on the published @somnia-chain/markets-sdk package pinned to 0.32.1 in package-lock.json. The read-only ABI declarations in packages/protocol/sdk-abi.ts are extracted from that pinned package; its MIT notice is retained in packages/protocol/SDK-LICENSE.txt. Other dependency licenses remain in their installed packages.

The project's domain, UI and journal implementation were written for DreamShield. No bot-kit strategy source was incorporated, and no claim is made that a bot-kit source commit was built or tested. The development guide's bot-kit references are research baselines, not an executed dependency. Before incorporating any future source from that repository, pin its exact commit and review/retain its license and attribution.

Product screenshots are captures of this project. No competitor branding or protected source was copied. The included source guide and review records are kept in the user's private repository and are not public disclosure authorization.
